## Voice stand-ins

Pack SNDINFO references DCY Dastard / Overdemoniac voice folders that are missing from this mix.
Dastard voices use DCY **Haunted** stand-ins; Overdemoniac voices use DCY **Oddity** stand-ins.
Projectile SFX for both are from the pack's `sounds/Projectiles/` set.
