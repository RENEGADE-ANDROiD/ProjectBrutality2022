The Hotel: Lost Contact — guest monsters + gore props

Source mod: **The Hotel: Lost Contact** (v4.8) by **Hevn_Cross-90eks** (ModDB).

Integrated combat classes
- `PB_HotelMory` — Imp-spawner guest (~2%). Sprites `MORY*`.
- `PB_HotelBloodCrawler` / `PB_HotelVoidCrawler` — Pinky-spawner guests (~2% each).
  Sprites `A4I*`/`HMDY` and `B4I*`/`BMDY`.
- `PB_HotelBloodCrawlerBig` — Baron-spawner guest (~2%). Sprites renamed `BOS2*` → `HTB2*`
  (avoids Hell Knight `BOS2` collision).
- `PB_HotelOgre` — ShotgunGuy-spawner guest (~3%). Sprites `OGR*`.
- `PB_HotelBruiser` — ChaingunGuy-spawner guest (~3%). Sprites renamed `BRUS*`/`BRUD*` →
  `HTBS*`/`HTBD*` (avoids BruiserDemon / Hell Knight death collisions).

Gore decorations (map-placeable only)
- `PB_HotelGorePile1`–`4` (`HLGR` A–D), `PB_HotelGoreHang1`–`4` (`HLGU` A–D).

Sprite / sound drops
- `SPRITES/Monsters/Hotel/` — combat PNGs (A4I* converted from Doom Picture via IWAD PLAYPAL).
- `SPRITES/Decoration/HotelGore/` — HLGR/HLGU.
- `sounds/monsters/Hotel/` — see `SNDINFO.txt` `hotel/*` / `mory/*` block.
- Re-extract helper: `maint_scripts/_extract_hotel_assets.py`.

Not folded
- Swat Zombies / raise corpses, SL1–3 bosses, DarkSerpent, poolwitch, roomstalker.
- Glory Kill `*GK` wrappers / PDA codex rows.

Cvars / ednums
- `pb_NoHotelMory`, `pb_NoHotelBloodCrawlerBig`, `pb_NoHotelOgre`, `pb_NoHotelBruiser`,
  `pb_NoHotelBloodCrawler`, `pb_NoHotelVoidCrawler` (+ `pb_disablenewenemies`).
- DoomEdNums `23285`–`23298` (combat + gore props).
