Backrooms Redux hunters — asset notes

Source mod: **Backrooms Redux: An Immersive Backrooms Experience** by **Kazim_Master**
(Doomworld / ModDB). Entity concepts also credit Backrooms Fandom Wiki authors.

Integrated classes
- `PB_BackroomsSanguineFestivus` — rare Baron-spawner guest (~2%). Sprites `SFVI` / `SFVW` / `SFVA`.
- `PB_BackroomsSmiler` — rare Hell Knight-spawner guest (~2%). Sprite `SMLR` (single Bright frame).

Sprite drops
- `SPRITES/Monsters/Backrooms/` — PNG lumps extracted from `BackroomsRedux.wad`:
  `SFVIA0` `SFVIB0` `SFVWA0` `SFVWB0` `SFVWC0` `SFVAA0` `SFVAB0` `SFVAC0` `SMLRA0`.

Sounds
- Folded from `BackroomsRedux.wad` into **`sounds/monsters/Backrooms/`**:
  - `RUN.mp3` → logical **`backrooms/run`** — Level ! chase bed (~1 min). Loop-started
    on `See` (CHAN_6), Melee/Pain return to `SeeChase` so the loop is not restarted,
    `A_StopSound(6)` on Death.
  - `EXPL.mp3` → logical **`backrooms/expl`** — death boom (`DeathSound`).
- Short alert/pain/melee still reuse PB/IWAD names (`knight/*` / `demon/*`, `baron/melee`,
  `weapons/gswing`). Source actors had no per-entity sight/pain/death lumps.
- Intentionally **not** folded: `RMUSS` / `RMUSR` (Level ! music beds, multi-MB),
  `SODA` / `GFBRK` (item / easter-egg cues unrelated to hunters).

Not folded (deferred)
- `PredatoryPylon` (SCP-173 stare-freeze) — unkillable LOS gimmick left out of this pass.

Glory Kill `*GK` wrappers and PDA codex rows are folded (`actors/Monsters/Backrooms/GloryKills.dec`). Unique silhouettes use pinata/stagger credit without family FPS fatality tokens.
